# LinkedIn Post

I’m excited to share **CivicPath**, an interactive election process assistant I built for the Google Antigravity challenge.

The idea is simple: voting information can feel scattered, deadline-heavy, and confusing, especially for first-time voters, people who recently moved, accessibility-focused voters, or anyone helping someone else understand the process.

CivicPath turns that into an easy-to-follow plan:

- personalized election timeline
- readiness score and next best action
- step-by-step checklist
- local assistant logic for common voter questions
- official-source guidance to avoid misinformation
- Google integrations with Gemini, Google Civic Information API, Calendar, Maps, and Cloud Run

I focused on making it practical, accessible, neutral, and lightweight enough for real-world use.

This was a great reminder that civic tech does not always need to be complex to be useful. Sometimes the highest-impact assistant is the one that makes the next step obvious.
